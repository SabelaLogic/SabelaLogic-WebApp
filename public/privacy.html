Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

SabelaLogic respects your privacy. We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with and what your rights are.

2. What we collect

  • Contact and identity details: your name, email address, mobile number and business name.
  • Project information: briefs, content, files and account access you give us to deliver a service.
  • Transaction information: what you bought, the amount, the date and the payment reference. Card payments are handled by our payment service provider (such as Payfast), and we do not receive or store full card numbers.
  • Voting platform information: the mobile number used for SMS one-time-PIN verification, your selections, timestamps and, only if you choose to give it, your name and marketing consent.
  • Technical information: basic logs such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security and to keep our services working.
  • Communications: messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, SMS or forms.

We do not knowingly collect special personal information, as defined in POPIA. Where a service might be used by children, for example voting at a family event, the organiser is responsible for getting any consent a competent person must give.

3. Why we use it (purpose and lawful basis)

  • To quote for, deliver and support the services or products you ask for, under a contract with you or steps towards one.
  • To process payments, keep accounting and tax records, and meet other legal obligations.
  • To verify voters and prevent fraud and duplicate or automated voting. This is in our and the event organiser’s legitimate interests and is part of running the service you are using.
  • To answer your enquiries and complaints.
  • To send you marketing, but only where POPIA allows it (see section 4).

4. Direct marketing

We send electronic marketing (SMS, WhatsApp or email) only to people who have opted in, or to existing customers about similar products or services. This follows section 69 of POPIA. Every marketing message tells you how to opt out, for example by replying STOP to an SMS. You can also opt out at any time by emailing info@[REDACTED].co.za. We stop sending marketing messages once you opt out.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where needed, with:

  • Operators who process it on our behalf under confidentiality and security obligations. These include hosting and database providers (for example Cloudflare, Supabase and Vercel), SMS gateways (for example BulkSMS), payment service providers (for example Payfast) and email providers.
  • Event organisers, for voting platforms. Organisers receive vote tallies and any information you chose to share with them when you voted.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

6. Transfers outside South Africa

Some of our service providers store or process data outside South Africa. When that happens, we use providers that are bound by data-protection laws, binding agreements or binding corporate rules that give protection substantially similar to POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires.

7. Security

We protect personal information with reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls, strong passwords and multi-factor authentication where available, and by limiting access to what each person or system needs. If we have reasonable grounds to believe your information has been accessed by an unauthorised person, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as section 22 of POPIA requires.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for. Accounting and tax records are kept for the periods the law requires, generally five years. Voting data, including verification data, is kept for up to 12 months after the event and is then deleted or anonymised, unless the organiser needs it longer for a legitimate dispute.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and ask for a record of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, excessive, out of date, incomplete, misleading or unlawfully obtained;
  • object to processing on reasonable grounds, and object to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent where we rely on consent; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To make a request, email info@[REDACTED].co.za with the subject “POPIA request”. We may need to confirm your identity first. Requests for records are also handled under the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000, where it applies.

10. Information Regulator

If you are not satisfied with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za · POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.

11. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies and similar technologies, for example to keep you signed in to the client portal and for security. We do not use advertising cookies. We use basic, privacy-respecting analytics provided by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) to understand overall site traffic and keep the site secure.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its effective date will always be on this page.
