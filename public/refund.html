Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel and how refunds work. It does not limit any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (“CPA”) or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (“ECTA”), where those Acts apply.

To request a refund or cancellation: email refund@sabelalogic.co.za with your name, payment reference, the date of payment and the reason. We will acknowledge your request within 2 business days and aim to resolve it within 14 business days.

1. ECTA cooling-off (online purchases)

For goods or services bought online, section 44 of ECTA may let you cancel without giving a reason and without penalty within 7 days after you receive the goods, or after the agreement for services is concluded. If you cancel this way, the only charge is the direct cost of returning the goods, and we refund you within 30 days. Section 42 of ECTA sets out exceptions. These include services that began with your consent before the 7 days ended, and goods made to your specifications or clearly personalised. Those exceptions apply to several of the items below.

2. Custom builds (websites, web apps, automation)

  • Before work starts: if you cancel before we start work, we refund your deposit in full, less any third-party costs already paid for you at your request, such as domain registration. We show these costs on your quote.
  • After work starts: the deposit pays for discovery, design and build time. If you cancel after work has started, we keep the part of the deposit that covers work already done. We send you a written breakdown and refund any unused balance. If the work done is worth more than the deposit, we may invoice the difference at the rates on your quote.
  • Milestone payments for completed and approved milestones are not refundable.
  • Defects: if a deliverable doesn’t match the agreed scope, we will fix it at no charge. If we can’t fix it, we will refund the part of the fee that relates to it.

3. Subscriptions, hosting and retainers

  • You can cancel at any time with 30 days’ written notice to refund@sabelalogic.co.za or support@sabelalogic.co.za. Service continues until the end of the period you have paid for.
  • We do not refund partly used billing periods, unless we fail to provide the service.
  • Fixed-term agreements follow the cancellation terms in the agreement and, where it applies, section 14 of the CPA.

4. Social media marketing packages

  • If you cancel before work on a package starts, you get a full refund.
  • Once work has started, for example when content has been created or a campaign scheduled, we refund only the part of the package that has not yet been delivered.
  • Amounts already paid to a platform for ad spend at your request cannot be recovered from the platform, so they are not refundable.
  • Results such as followers, reach or sales are not guaranteed, so a shortfall in results is not on its own grounds for a refund.

5. Paid votes on voting platforms

  • A paid vote is a digital service delivered immediately at your request. Once your vote is recorded, it is non-refundable and cannot be transferred.
  • Exceptions: we will refund you in full if
    • the event or the voting is cancelled before results are finalised;
    • you were charged but your vote was not recorded because of a technical error; or
    • you were charged more than once for the same vote.
  • Votes cancelled because they are fraudulent or break the published voting rules are not refunded, unless the law requires a refund.
  • Send refund requests for paid votes within 7 days of the voting period closing. Include the mobile number you used and your payment reference.

6. Merchandise

  • Sizing and exchanges: please check the size guide before ordering. If the size doesn’t fit, we will try to exchange it for another size, subject to stock, if the item is returned unworn and in its original condition within 7 days of collection or delivery.
  • Defective or incorrect items: the CPA gives you an implied warranty of quality for six months. If an item is defective or not what you ordered, you may choose a repair, a replacement or a refund. Items collected in person should be checked at collection, but this does not take away your CPA rights.
  • Delivery or collection: if we can’t supply an ordered item, or the event where collection was arranged is cancelled and we can’t offer reasonable alternative collection or delivery that you accept, we refund you in full.
  • Items made to your specifications or clearly personalised, such as with your name, are not returnable unless they are defective.

7. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid using the original payment method where possible, or by EFT to an account in the payer’s name. Refunds are processed within 30 days, and usually much sooner. We do not charge you a fee to process a refund.

8. Complaints

If you’re unhappy with a refund decision, email support@sabelalogic.co.za or call or WhatsApp +27 74 842 4596. Our complaints process and your other options are set out in our Terms & Conditions.
